                                   🧠 General Knowledge Quiz

A lightweight, interactive command-line quiz application built in Python. Test your knowledge across multiple domains, get instant feedback, and see how you rank!

---

## ✨ Features

* **Interactive CLI Interface:** Greets the player by name and guides them through the quiz seamlessly.
* **Curated Question Bank:** Includes 10 diverse questions covering geography, science, literature, history, and pop culture.
* **Robust Input Validation:** Gracefully handles invalid inputs (such as non-numeric entries or out-of-range options) to prevent application crashes.
* **Instant Feedback:** Displays correct or incorrect visual indicators ($\checkmark$ / $\times$) immediately after each answer, revealing the correct choice if you miss one.
* **Performance Evaluation:** Calculates your final score and percentage, providing customized encouraging remarks based on your grade tier.

---

## 🛠️ Prerequisites

Make sure you have **Python 3.x** installed on your machine. You can verify your installation by running:

```bash
